INSIDE THE HOMES

Everyday details,
planned in from the start.

A good apartment is judged on ordinary days: how the morning light falls, where the shoes go, whether the kitchen works for two people at once. We plan the details that make those moments easier.

  • Rooms proportioned for real furniture, not just a tidy floor plan
  • Cross-ventilation and daylight planned into living areas and bedrooms
  • Storage built into the layout — wardrobes, kitchen utility and an entry zone
  • Concealed electrical wiring with sensible placement of points and switches
  • Parking, lift access and security considered from day one

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know
before you enquire.

Quick answers to what most homebuyers ask first. For anything else, the team is a call or message away.

Which home sizes do you offer?

Every project concept on this site offers 2 BHK and 3 BHK apartments. Buildings are kept small, with roughly 8–10 flats each, so communities stay quiet and manageable.

Are the homes ready to move into?

It depends on the project. Willow Residences, Maple Heights and Sunrise Apartments are shown as ready, while The Orchid is under construction. Each project page lists its current status.

Where are your projects located?

Project concepts are spread across Chennai, including Sholinganallur, OMR, Velachery and Green Park. Location details are placeholders in this prototype and will be replaced with verified addresses.

How do I arrange a site visit?

Send an enquiry through the contact page or call the Chennai office. The team is available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Will I get pricing and specification details?

Project pages list indicative specifications. Pricing, inclusions and documentation are shared directly when you enquire, so you receive current, accurate information.
