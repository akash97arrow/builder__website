BUYER GUIDE

What to compare
before you choose.

Configuration is only one part of the decision. Consider location, usable area, light and ventilation, parking, water, security, connectivity and the builder’s specifications.

Location

Look beyond the pin. Think about commute routes, schools, healthcare, groceries and daily services.

Layout

Check how much of the home is genuinely usable and whether rooms support your family's routine.

Specifications

Review materials, fittings, utilities, security provisions and maintenance considerations carefully.

2 BHK OR 3 BHK?

Choosing the home
that fits your life.

Both configurations are available across our project concepts. Here is a simple way to think about the choice.

2 BHK Apartment

A compact, efficient layout with two bedrooms, a living and dining area and a kitchen.

  • Well suited to couples, small families and first-time buyers
  • Easier to furnish, clean and maintain
  • A sensible choice if you want a prime location at a manageable budget

3 BHK Apartment

A larger layout with three bedrooms, giving the household room to grow and adapt.

  • Ideal for growing families or parents living together
  • A spare room can become a home office, guest room or study
  • More space for storage, guests and flexible daily routines

PROJECT QUESTIONS

Things buyers
often ask.

Short answers to common project questions. The team can share more detail on request.

What is the difference between “Ready” and “Under Construction”?

A ready project is complete and can be visited as it stands. An under-construction project is still being built, so you review plans and specifications and follow progress until handover.

Can I switch between 2 BHK and 3 BHK at a project?

Both configurations are offered in each project concept. Availability of specific flats is confirmed when you enquire.

Do the projects have the same amenities?

The core amenities are consistent across projects. See the amenities page for the full list, and each project page for specifics.

How accurate are the details on this website?

This is a prototype and project details are placeholders. They will be replaced with verified information before launch.
