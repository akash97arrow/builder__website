  • When you hope to move in

Helpful to bring

CONTACT QUESTIONS

Before you
get in touch.

A few practical answers about enquiries and visits.

Is there any obligation when I enquire or visit?

No. Enquiries and site visits are meant to help you gather information. You decide if and when to take the next step.

Can I visit outside office hours or on Sundays?

Our regular hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. If you need another time, mention it in your message and the team will do their best to help.

Can my family come along?

Please do. Homes are lived in by the whole household, so we encourage everyone who will use the home to visit.

Can I get information by phone or email instead of visiting?

Yes. Share your questions by phone or email and the team can send project details before you decide to visit.
