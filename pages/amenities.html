AMENITY QUESTIONS

Common questions
about amenities.

A few points buyers often ask about. Project-specific answers are shared when you enquire.

Are the amenities the same in every project?

The core list is consistent: parking, lift, security, water supply, power backup, landscaping and CCTV. Exact inclusions can vary by project, so each project page and your enquiry response will confirm what applies.

What does power backup cover?

Power backup is planned to support essential common-area services such as lifts, lighting and water pumping. Coverage inside individual apartments will be confirmed per project.

Is there parking for visitors?

Visitor parking is part of our parking planning. The number of spaces and how they are allocated is confirmed for each project.

Who maintains the common areas?

Maintenance arrangements and likely charges are explained before you commit, so there are no surprises after handover. Details will be finalised with verified builder information.
