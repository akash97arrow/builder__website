BUYER CHECKLIST

Questions worth asking
any builder.

We would rather you ask us hard questions than discover answers later. Use this list with us, or with anyone else you are considering.

Ask Us These →
  • What exactly is included in the specification — flooring, fittings, doors, windows, electrical?
  • Which approvals and documents are available, and when can I review them?
  • How is parking allocated, and where do visitors park?
  • What are the arrangements for water supply and power backup?
  • Who maintains common areas after handover, and what will it likely cost?
  • How much natural light and ventilation does my specific flat get through the day?
  • Who do I call after handover, and how quickly can I expect a response?

SMALL BY DESIGN

Why smaller buildings
make better homes.

With roughly 8–10 flats per building, the whole experience of living and buying changes.

Fewer neighbours, closer community

Smaller buildings tend to be quieter and friendlier, with shared spaces that feel like your own rather than a crowd.

Easier upkeep

Fewer flats mean simpler maintenance, more manageable common costs and quicker decisions about repairs.

More personal attention

A small portfolio means buyers speak with people who know their building and their flat by name.
